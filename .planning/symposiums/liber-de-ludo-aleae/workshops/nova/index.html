Glossary

circuitus
Sample space — every equally possible outcome.
aequitas
Equal conditions of die, table, light, stakes, knowledge.
r : s
Favorable ways to unfavorable ways. Fair price matches this.
EV
Expected value. Fair game ⇒ 0.

Reasoning On The Mean: treating n×p as a probability.

Cardano’s leftover name for luck as a person. We fire it; we keep the lean detector.

Knowledge (a method, over many casts) vs luck (one favorable throw).

Nova · 2

Equal conditions first

Aequitas is the principle: opponent, bystanders, money, situation, box, die. Probability is how you check that the table is even — not a carnival trick.

A bake-off that hands one model the answers is a marked deck, whatever the score says.

Nova · 3 · chapter 14

Count the whole circuit

The circuit is every face. Favorable ways are r. The rest are s. Lay the wager in that proportion.

Tiny r : s calculator

Try. Default is 27:25 (sums 10 vs 9 on three dice). See. p, fair odds, even-money EV, and p². Why. p² is how you repeat a trial. You do not square the odds.

Nova · 4

Refuse ROTM

ROTM says 3 × 1/6 = ½. The circuit says 1 − (5/6)³ ≈ 0.421. Four throws to cross a half.

False claim

Add one-sixth per try until you hit certainty.

That is ROTM. At n=6 it claims certainty. A six can still fail forever.

Circuit

Independence: chance of missing every time is (5/6)^n. Subtract from 1.

At n=3 that is 1 − 125/216 ≈ 0.421. Four throws to cross a half.

Never treat the count you expect (n×p) as the chance it happens (P).

Nova · 5

Lean is not luck

If outcomes systematically lean one way, there is a reason — bias, fraud, or a misspecified circuit. Keep that detector. Fire the Prince.

A score that always “just equals what it should be” is as suspicious as one that always leans.

Nova · 6

Pay remaining work

Race to 6, stopped at 5–3. B still needs three wins in a row. The eight equally likely last-three sequences: only BBB gives B the match (1/8). The other seven give A. Pot 7:1.

An interrupted job should not credit tokens already burned.

A : B = 0 : 0

Nova · 7

Small stakes, named ruin

Equal money at risk is not equal chance of going broke. A rich purse and a thin purse on the same stake are not an even table. Ruin is vice when the bet is the treasury.

A stop-loss (leave the table) is not a sample space (count the faces).
