**Audience:** WeftOS / clawft engineers, governance, MetaHarness operators.
**Length:** ~14 interactive slides (not bound to 10). 20–25 minutes with exploration.
**Thesis:** A score that does not name its circuit is a wager dressed as measurement.
**Design:** DESIGN.md — `bg_app #08080A`, `accent #C4A25C`, `ok #6EC896`, `warn #DCAF55`, `crit #DC5F5F`. Calm chrome, loud signals. Interactive depth on every content slide.
