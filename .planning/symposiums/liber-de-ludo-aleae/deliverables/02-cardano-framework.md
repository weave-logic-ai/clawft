# Cardano framework — named primitives

Locked by Room Nova. Coniunctio may not proliferate synonyms.

| Cardano | Modern | Forbidden blur |
|---------|--------|----------------|
| *circuitus* | sample space | circuit-breaker |
| *aequitas* | equal conditions | EffectVector.fairness vibe |
| *r : s* | fair price / EV=0 | multiplying odds |
| *scientia* / *fortuna* | model / residual | luck-as-skill |
| systematic lean | calibration / bias / fraud | Prince |
| *pⁿ* | independence, stated | ROTM |
| *np* | frequency over large n | one demo |
| fraud catalog | hidden take-rate | method of play |
| remaining points | continuation value | sunk score |
| small stakes | ruin bound | treasury on one cast |

Decision checklist:

1. Name the circuit or say incomplete:why.
2. Are conditions equal (info, tools, eval, stake)?
3. What is favorable? What are odds?
4. What is the stake and the ruin?
5. Is the offered price fair, or is there edge?
6. How many casts (*n*) justify the claim type?
7. If the match stopped, split by remaining work.
